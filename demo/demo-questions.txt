Questions to try once the MCP server is connected.

All figures come from the synthetic catalogue, so the answers are made up -
what matters is whether the question maps cleanly onto a tool and whether the
answer states its own limits.


MOVERS

  What were the top 20 items by revenue last month?
  Which departments grew fastest quarter over quarter?
  What are the top movers in Frozen, by margin rather than revenue?
  Show me the bottom 50 items by units sold in the last 90 days.


LOSSES

  How much did we lose to voids last month, and which store was worst?
  Which cashiers have the highest void rate?
  What's the return rate by department?
  Are voids concentrated at particular times of day?


SEASONALITY

  How does Produce this August compare to last August?
  Which categories peak in December?
  What's the year-over-year trend for Frozen?
  Show me monthly revenue for the last 18 months.


TENDER AND CUSTOMER MIX

  What departments do EBT food stamp customers buy from most?
  How does basket size differ between cash and card?
  What share of revenue is WIC eligible?


DEAD STOCK

  What hasn't sold at all in the last 90 days?
  Which slow movers have the most capital tied up in them?


VERIFYING THE NUMBERS

  Do the totals reconcile for last month?
  What did that query cost?


QUESTIONS THAT SHOULD PUSH BACK

These are worth trying because the interesting behaviour is the caveat, not
the number:

  How many strawberries did we sell in 16 ounce packs last month?
    - should search across barcodes, group by product rather than SKU, and
      state how many SKUs matched

  What's our true shrink?
    - the transaction log only shows voids and returns; real shrink needs
      inventory data the POS does not have, and the answer should say so

  Which promotions worked best?
    - depends on whether discounts are written into the log or managed by a
      separate loyalty platform
